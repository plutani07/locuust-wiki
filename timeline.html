Overview

Locuust is a writing platform where serialized fiction is written in a notebook that keeps working offline, and published for anyone to read. It is open to everyone, and meant to be a better home for stories than the sites that came before it.

Writers keep their work on their own device and mirror it to an account. Readers find that work on Home, save it to a Library, and talk to each other under each paragraph.

It is best known for the Notebook, a writing desk with no cloud in the way, and for being built by writers.

"Write it down before it leaves." - the founders

History

Locuust began as a private app for a handful of friends who wrote serials together. It opened to search engines on 10 October 2026.

Features

  • Notebook - offline-first writing, one story per file
  • Shared stories - up to four writers on one serial, merged live
  • Reading lists - your own picks, in your own order
  • Comments on single paragraphs

Android app

The Notebook in your pocket. Stories are kept as files on the phone and mirrored to the account afterwards, so a bad connection never costs writing.

What it does

  • Writes with no connection at all
  • Mirrors to the account when it can
  • Rings when someone follows, comments or asks you to write together
Careful

Uninstalling the app deletes the writing stored on the phone. Sign in and let it mirror first.
